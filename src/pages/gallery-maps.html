The Dam
Celestial's HEBO-class hydroelectric dam surveys a world from orbit, intelligently chooses the best location for a dam, then drops out of orbit and deploys itself, all without human supervision. The twin reservoirs' overflow doors are fail-safe, opening and releasing dangerous excess water should the power completely fail.

FOREST
Weather Control
The powerful plasma storms that accompany near-surface bohrium mining can be prevented through the use of PITRE's synoptic effector technology, AKA weather control towers. Like many of the megastructures on Shear, the towers are self-maintaining and self-repairing, designed to run smoothly with a crew of only a few dozen.

FOREST
Fusion Plant
A Celestial Fission-Electric Type IV power plant provides electrical power to nearby settlements. It orbits the planet looking for a naturally-occurring thorium deposit, then lands and begins mining and processing the ore it needs to power the steam turbines. Its crew of 22 maintains the plant and operates the control systems.

LAVA
Colonial Water & Power
One of the largest man-made structures on Shear, CWP routes all power generated at the fusion plant and hydroelectric plant, as well as all freshwater. Built over a hydrologically active geothermal site, the plant is self-powered and self-sustaining. It has to be, as all life in the colony depends on its water and energy.
